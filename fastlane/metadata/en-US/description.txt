You are a star. Absorb anything smaller than you, and stay away from anything bigger.

Touch the screen to move: every push throws a little of your mass behind you, so each move costs you size. Grow carefully, think about momentum, and let gravity work for you.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• A campaign that keeps adding new objects: black holes with orbiting planets, supernovas, pulsars, comets, wormholes, binary stars, antimatter and bombs.
• A daily level — the same for every player, every day.
• A sandbox to build your own universe.
• Replays of your best runs and a predicted trajectory to plan your path.
• Three playfield sizes, from compact to huge.

MADE FOR LISTENING
Generative ambient music changes with every world. Nearby stars sing: small ones high, big ones low, from the side of the screen where they are. Play with headphones.

NO PRESSURE
No ads, no accounts, no internet needed. Progress stays on your device. One finger is all you need.
