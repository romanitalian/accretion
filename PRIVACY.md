## Who we are

**Accretion** (“the app”) is a space game for **iPhone and iPad**. You play as a star that absorbs smaller stars and avoids bigger ones. The game works fully **offline**.

This policy describes how information is handled when you use the app.

## Information we do not collect

- We **do not** collect, store, or share any personal data.
- We **do not** require an account or sign-in.
- We **do not** ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.
- We **do not** use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- The app makes **no network requests**. We do not operate a backend.

## Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app stores data **locally on your device**:

- **Game progress** — completed levels, stars, unlocked looks, and achievements
- **Replays** — the inputs of your best runs, so they can be watched again
- **Settings** — volume, controls, playfield size, and similar preferences

This data stays on your device inside the app’s sandbox. **We cannot access it.** Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Sharing

If you use the **Share** button on the result screen, the system share sheet opens with an image and text of your result. What happens next is up to you and the app you share to; nothing is sent to us.

## Purchases

The app has **no in-app purchases** and **no subscriptions**.

## Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your rights (EEA, UK, and similar regions)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. The app holds no personal data on our systems: everything stays on your device. For any request, contact **rmndevapps@gmail.com**.

## California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as those terms are defined in California privacy law.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The **Last updated** date at the top will change when we do. If a future version collects any data, this policy and the App Privacy section of the App Store listing will be updated first.
