# Accretion — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Accretion does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
- No network access: the game works fully offline.
- Game progress, settings and replays are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/romanitalian/accretion/issues.
